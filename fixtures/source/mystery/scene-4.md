The west vault crouched behind the cellar stair, iron-faced and older than the house above it. Dr. Hale, who had examined the body before first light, met them at the vault door with the constable. The vault lock took the key in two turns, once left to lift the wards, once right to draw the bolt.

Inside lay ledgers, deeds, and a new will, signed and witnessed a month ago, that named no nephew at all.

Somewhere above them Bruno's bark rolled down the corridor, and Mara read the will twice before she spoke.

As for the groundskeeper, the constable was plain on the matter: Tomas Reyes had spent that same hour at the village forge, and half the village could swear to it.
