Mrs. Brandt received her in the conservatory, among orchids Tomas Reyes was repotting that same hour at the far bench. Dr. Hale had come an hour after dawn, she said, and nothing in the study had been moved before he arrived.

Mara asked about the key. "The brass key opens the west vault," Brandt said. "Edmund trusted no bank." The vault lock takes the key in two turns, once left to lift the wards, once right to draw the bolt. Brandt had watched Edmund work it a hundred times and had never once held the key herself.

It was Mara who found it. Kneeling where the carpet's corner sat proud of the boards, she pried up the loose floorboard and lifted the brass key from the dark beneath, cold and unhurried in her palm. Only Brandt saw her do it, and Mara asked her to keep it so.
