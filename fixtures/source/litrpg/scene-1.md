Rook woke under the toll road's third arch with the System's pale script already hanging in the dark. The debt to Marrow comes due at the toll gate, it reminded him, as if he could forget owing that particular man anything.

The System kept its one iron rule where bodies were concerned: no draught nor blessing carries a body past its ceiling. And its second, where souls were concerned: levels once granted are not recalled, short of dying. Everything else, Rook had learned, was negotiable.
