The outer gate took its cut like everything else on the toll road. Rook paid the gatekeeper five gold at the wicket and got a chalk mark on his sleeve for it, good for one passage and no refunds.

Beyond the gate the braziers ran short of the wall, and the gap between light and stone was exactly the kind of dark the lantern was for. He left it unlit. Rook slipped between the braziers on Shadowstep and the watchman saw nothing, not even the chalk mark.
