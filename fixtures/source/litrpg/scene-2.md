The night market under the aqueduct sold everything a debtor might need and nothing he could afford. Rook stopped at a barrelhead stall where an old woman sold light.

He counted twenty gold onto the barrel for a storm lantern, glass thick as a knuckle, and she threw in a strap of cured leather for nothing because, she said, he had the look of a man walking somewhere dark.

You feel stronger, the System offered, apropos of nothing. No number anywhere had moved.
